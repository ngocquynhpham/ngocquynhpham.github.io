How I think

P.I.S.I

— the same four questions, every time.

Every case study on this page — MyHappyClass, HRM, E-Signature, EconWiki — is broken down the same way. Not because it's a template, but because it's actually how I approach a new feature.

01

Problem

What's actually broken, and who feels it. Not "what feature is missing" — the specific moment it goes wrong for someone.

02

Insight

What the data, the user, or the business logic actually reveals once you look past the original request.

03

Solution

The smallest thing that fixes the real problem — not the biggest thing that could theoretically fix it too.

04

Impact

What changed, measured against the exact thing that was broken in step one. If there's no real number yet, this stays honest about that too.

You'll see this exact structure in every project below.
